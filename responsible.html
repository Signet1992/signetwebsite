Responsible Production

Production held to
a higher standard.

Responsible production at Signet Enterprises Limited is embedded in how we work — in the relationships we build, the standards we hold our factories to, and the genuine commitment we make to every person in our supply chain.

Our Commitment
Beyond the requirement.

Responsible production at Signet Enterprises Limited is part of our culture, not an add-on to our process. It is embedded in how we work — in the relationships we build with factories, the standards we hold them to, and the genuine commitment we make to the people who make our products.

We hold ourselves and our factory partners to standards that go beyond what compliance requires — because we believe the right way to produce is defined by what a responsible business chooses to do, not simply what the law demands.

These principles apply equally across all four Signet brands and every factory in our network — without exception.

Our Approach
Continuous improvement.

We work continuously with factory partners to raise ethical and environmental standards year on year. Certification is our starting point. Our goal is always to go further — setting progressive targets, investing in factory development and building the kind of long-term relationships that make genuine improvement possible.

Full compliance documentation, audit records and supply chain mapping are provided to clients as standard across all sectors. We treat compliance as a live commitment — managed every day, not reviewed periodically.

Our Framework
Four pillars of responsible production.
01
Ethical Labour Standards
We are committed to fair, safe and respectful working conditions across every facility in our network. All factories must meet or exceed ILO core labour conventions — and we work actively with partners to raise standards beyond the minimum requirement.
  • Prohibition of forced and child labour
  • Fair wages and legal working hours
  • Safe and healthy working environments
  • Freedom of association and collective bargaining
  • Anti-discrimination policies in place
02
Environmental Responsibility
We work with factories committed to reducing their environmental impact — setting targets that go beyond regulatory requirements and tracking progress year on year. Environmental performance is part of every factory review across all four sectors.
  • Water consumption monitoring and reduction targets
  • Restricted substances list (RSL) compliance
  • Waste reduction and recycling programmes
  • Carbon footprint tracking across production
  • Sustainable fabric options across all categories
03
Compliance & Visibility
Our clients have clear, direct visibility of their production at every stage. Full compliance documentation, audit reports and supply chain mapping are provided as standard — not on request. Compliance is a live commitment, managed continuously across all sectors.
  • Full tier-1 and tier-2 factory disclosure
  • Regular audit reports provided to clients
  • SEDEX membership across core factories
  • Corrective action plans tracked and resolved
  • Compliance documentation aligned to client standards
04
Continuous Improvement
Certification is a starting point, not an end state. We work alongside factory partners to raise ethical and environmental performance over time — investing in long-term relationships and setting progressive targets that go beyond what any certification requires.
  • Annual factory development plans
  • Training and capacity building programmes
  • Long-term factory partnerships prioritised
  • Worker welfare initiatives actively supported
  • Progressive targets set and reviewed annually

Certifications
Certified across every sector.

Certification confirms the baseline. Our commitment goes further. These accreditations apply across our factory network and reflect the minimum standard our partners are expected to meet.

OEKO-TEX STEP
Sustainable textile production certification covering social and environmental responsibility — verifying both product safety and factory conditions across our supply chain.
SEDEX
Supplier Ethical Data Exchange membership — enabling transparent sharing of ethical supply chain data, audit reports and compliance documentation with our clients.
BSCI
Business Social Compliance Initiative — social auditing across our factory network against internationally recognised standards for labour rights and working conditions.
Fair Wear Foundation
Member of the Fair Wear Foundation — committed to improving labour conditions for garment workers through verification, training and continuous improvement programmes.
OEKO-TEX 100
Tested for harmful substances — certified fabrics available across all product categories. Every component tested, not just the finished garment.
Bluesign
Responsible chemistry and resource efficiency in fabric production — available across our technical and performance fabric range in applicable sectors.
