Quality Assurance
A preventive QA system.
Not just a final check.
Stage 1
In-Line Inspection
Quality checks conducted during production — at critical construction stages — before issues can compound. Problems are identified and corrected on the production floor, not after.
Stage 2
Pre-Final Inspection
A comprehensive inspection of finished garments before packing begins — covering measurements, construction, appearance and labelling against the approved sample and tech pack.
Stage 3
AQL Final Inspection
Random sampling to internationally recognised AQL standards on packed, ready-to-ship goods. Full inspection reports provided to clients as standard with every production run.

Our quality approach is preventive, not reactive. By managing quality at every stage of production — not just the end — we ensure issues are caught early, costs are controlled and delivery dates are protected.

📐
Measurement Verification
All critical measurements checked against approved specs at multiple production stages.
🧵
Construction Standards
Seam strength, stitch density, trim attachment and construction integrity checked throughout.
🧪
Lab Testing
Fabric and garment testing against relevant standards — wash fastness, tensile strength, chemical compliance and more.
Responsible Production
Production held to a higher standard.

We hold our factory network to standards that go beyond certification — treating compliance as a live commitment, not a periodic exercise.

Ethical Labour Standards
All factories meet ILO core labour conventions — fair wages, safe working conditions, freedom of association and prohibition of forced and child labour.
Environmental Responsibility
Water management, chemical controls, waste reduction and carbon tracking across all production facilities — with annual improvement targets.
Compliance & Transparency
Full audit records, supply chain mapping and compliance documentation provided to clients as standard — not on request.
Continuous Improvement
We work alongside factory partners to raise standards year on year. Certification is our baseline — our goal is always to go further.
