Who We Are
Signet Enterprises Limited

Signet Enterprises Limited was founded in London in 1992 with a straightforward conviction — that global brands deserved a production partner with real knowledge of the product, real presence in the factories, and real accountability for what came out the other end.

We are a specialist garment production group, headquartered in Dhaka with offices across the UK, Hong Kong, UAE, Bangladesh and Pakistan. Across five offices and four sectors, 160 people apply the same standard every day — one that has been refined and proven over more than three decades.

We have developed expertise across four unique sectors of the garment industry — workwear, activewear, apparel and lifestyle. Each sector has its own specialist team and product knowledge, but all share the same foundational values: permanent in-country teams, rigorous production standards, and a long-term commitment to the people and factories we work with.

What we stand for.

Six values that have defined Signet Enterprises Limited since 1992 — and that apply equally across every sector, every office and every production run.

Quality Without Compromise
Every production run is held to a standard that protects the brand behind it. Quality is managed in-line — not inspected at the end. Issues are resolved in production, not discovered in the warehouse.
Respect Across the Supply Chain
Respect means honouring the standards our clients set, meeting test requirements and shipment dates, protecting the value of our clients' brands, and treating every factory partner with fairness. It means adding value at every stage — and genuinely understanding the challenges of everyone we work with.
Permanent Presence
Our in-country teams in Bangladesh and Pakistan are permanently embedded in the factories — not occasional visitors. That permanence is what makes genuine accountability possible.
Long-Term Thinking
We invest in long-term relationships — with clients, with factories and with our own people. We prioritise partners who share our values, and we measure success over years, not seasons.
Specialist Expertise
Each of our four sectors is led by a specialist team with deep product knowledge. We do not try to be all things — we focus on what we know, and we do it properly.
Responsible Standards
Compliance is our baseline, not our ceiling. We work continuously with factory partners to raise ethical and environmental standards — because responsible production is a commitment, not a certificate.
